Welcome to **BytheByte**, a place for notes on code, ideas, and things worth figuring out.

The idea is simple: not everything needs to be a definitive guide. Sometimes a small discovery, a useful snippet, or a question explored out loud is enough.

## What you'll find here

- **Things learned** while building, reading, and experimenting.
- **Technical notes** that might save someone else a little time.
- **Ideas in progress**, because thinking is better when it's shared.

Small posts, thoughtful details, and room to change your mind. That's the spirit of this space.
